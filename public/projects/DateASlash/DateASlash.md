
<h2 style="text-align:center;">About the Project</h2>

<p align="justify">
<i>Date a Slash</i> is a 2D <b>Rhythm Action Game</b> developed in collaboration with Overscoped Studio. In this quirky and fast-paced title, players control William, a college student caught up in the chaotic schemes of his eccentric classmate Clara. The core gameplay revolves around inputting precise <i>action commands</i> through a <i>command block</i>, synchronized with the rhythm of the music.
</p>

<p align="justify">
I contributed to the project as the <b>Gameplay Programmer</b>. My responsibilities included:
<ul>
<li>Implementing the action command system, allowing players to execute timed inputs through the command block interface.</li>
<li>Developing rhythm synchronization systems that align gameplay actions with the song’s BPM (beats per minute).</li>
<li>Designing and implementing a JSON-based beatmap system for level layouts, enabling song-specific enemy and beat placement.</li>
<li>Creating an endless mode system that procedurally generates enemy patterns and beat events based on tempo and difficulty progression.</li>
</ul>
